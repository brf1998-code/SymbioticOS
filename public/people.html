People

Who works here, so a request comes back to the person who made it.
How it works. Each device stays signed in with the company's floor or manager password, as before. On top of that, a person taps their name on the feedback button and types a short PIN, so their requests are theirs on any tablet or phone: they can see where each one is under "My requests", the page says who asked when a change goes live, and they are asked whether it fixed it. The board shows who asked and who approved. Nobody has to: a device with no one signed in still works and says "anonymous". A PIN is shown once, here, when you add someone or reset it. Five wrong tries lock a name for five minutes.
